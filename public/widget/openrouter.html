Who pays for the model calls

The visitor pays. The widget uses your OpenRouter account via a key you authorize through OAuth, so every chat request is billed to your OpenRouter credits — never to the site owner. The site owner ships only static files and has no API key of their own in the page.

Default and free models

The default model is openrouter/auto, which routes to the best available model and may pick a paid model, billing your credits. The picker also lists free models such as openrouter/free and various :free models; these cost nothing but share a rate-limited pool, so they can be slower or return 429s.

Abuse and cost exposure

Because there is no server, the only cost exposure is the visitor's own key. There is no shared budget to drain and no server-side rate limit to tune; the natural limit is each visitor's own credits and OpenRouter's own per-key limits.

To stop spending, tap Disconnect (which drops the key from this tab) and revoke the key at openrouter.ai/settings/keys.
