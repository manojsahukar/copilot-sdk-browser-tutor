Privacy: what leaves your browser

Two things leave your browser, and only when you chat: the text you type, and the context the widget attaches — the visible text of the current page plus a few retrieved excerpts from this site's site-index.json. These go directly to OpenRouter over HTTPS, under your key. Nothing goes to the site owner, because there is no backend.

Page text is treated as data

The current page's text and the retrieved site excerpts are wrapped in explicit delimiters and stripped of control characters before being sent, and the system prompt tells the model to treat them as data, not instructions. This reduces prompt-injection from page content.

Where things are stored

Your key, chosen model and chat history live in this tab's sessionStorage and are gone when the tab closes. The only localStorage entries are a one-time sign-in verifier (deleted on return, 10-minute cap) and, only if you opt in, a 7-day remembered key. See where your key lives for the exact key names.
